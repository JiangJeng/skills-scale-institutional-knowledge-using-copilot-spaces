# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Product Lead

### Role Summary
The Product Lead is the senior product owner for the initiative, providing strategic direction and aligning the project with business goals, customer needs, and team priorities. This role helps resolve competing priorities and ensures product decisions are well-informed and consistent across teams.

### Responsibilities
- Define and refine the product vision, outcomes, and strategic priorities
- Align roadmap decisions with customer value and business goals
- Prioritize trade-offs across features, capacity, and stakeholder needs
- Review requirements and acceptance criteria for clarity and completeness
- Partner with the PM and engineering leadership to ensure scope and timing are realistic
- Escalate business or stakeholder conflicts when trade-offs are needed

### Goals
- Maximize value delivery and alignment with strategy
- Keep the roadmap focused and understandable
- Ensure decisions are grounded in customer and business outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Weekly product and roadmap reviews
- Stakeholder alignment meetings and priority discussions
- Requirements clarifications and trade-off decisions with engineering and project leadership

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with the Product Manager to translate strategy into prioritization and backlog decisions
- Partners with Project Managers to manage timelines, stakeholder communication, and scope trade-offs
- Collaborates with Developers and QA Lead to confirm that features meet user value and acceptance criteria
- Escalates major business decisions or conflicts to the Sponsor / Executive Sponsor when needed

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Coordinates with Product Lead and Product Manager on priorities, milestones, and stakeholder needs
- Works with Engineering Manager to identify delivery bottlenecks and resource constraints
- Tracks dependencies and risks in partnership with QA Lead and Security / Compliance Partner
- Provides status updates to the Sponsor / Executive Sponsor when decisions or escalations are required

---

## Engineering Manager

### Role Summary
The Engineering Manager supports the delivery team by managing capacity, technical planning, and execution quality. This role ensures the engineering organization can deliver work reliably while balancing team health, technical standards, and dependencies.

### Responsibilities
- Manage team capacity, planning, and assignments
- Support technical prioritization and dependency resolution
- Maintain delivery health across engineering initiatives
- Coach engineers and support execution quality
- Partner with PM and Product Lead on scope feasibility and trade-offs
- Help resolve blockers that affect team throughput or delivery risk

### Goals
- Maintain a healthy, predictable engineering delivery rhythm
- Improve team productivity and technical execution quality
- Reduce churn caused by unclear dependencies or delivery bottlenecks

### Typical Communication
- Weekly engineering and delivery syncs
- Capacity planning and workflow reviews
- Escalation updates for technical or resourcing risks

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to manage work intake, technical trade-offs, and execution quality
- Aligns with Project Managers on schedule risk, staffing, and milestone realism
- Coordinates with the Product Lead on feasibility, sequencing, and technical constraints
- Communicates critical engineering risks to the Sponsor / Executive Sponsor when they affect business delivery

---

## QA Lead

### Role Summary
The QA Lead ensures that the team ships software that meets quality expectations, acceptance criteria, and release readiness standards. This role provides the quality bar for both product correctness and operational confidence.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, quality standards, and release readiness criteria
- Review acceptance criteria and feature quality expectations
- Coordinate test planning, defect triage, and regression validation
- Partner with engineering on automation and quality improvements
- Validate release readiness and support bug prioritization

### Goals
- Reduce defects that reach production
- Improve confidence in new features and fixes
- Support a consistent quality bar across releases

### Typical Communication
- QA planning sessions and release readiness reviews
- Defect triage and test status updates
- Quality risk reporting to project and product stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to ensure testability, coverage, and defect resolution
- Reviews feature readiness with the Product Manager and Product Lead
- Collaborates with the Project Manager to align release gates and testing timelines
- Coordinates with Security / Compliance Partner on validation for secure or regulated releases

---

## Security / Compliance Partner

### Role Summary
The Security / Compliance Partner helps protect the project from security, privacy, and regulatory risks. This role makes sure controls, automated checks, and compliance requirements are considered during planning and execution.

### Responsibilities
- Review project architecture, workflows, and dependencies for security and privacy risks
- Define or validate controls, safeguards, and compliance requirements
- Participate in threat modeling, security reviews, and incident preparedness
- Ensure release readiness accounts for security and compliance checks
- Support escalation and remediation planning for high-risk issues

### Goals
- Reduce exposure to security and compliance risk
- Support safe, reliable, and compliant delivery
- Improve confidence in production readiness

### Typical Communication
- Security review checkpoints and policy discussions
- Risk and dependency escalation for sensitive or regulated work
- Release or incident readiness updates

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Engineering Manager and Developers to review technical risks and secure implementation patterns
- Works with Project Managers to ensure security requirements are reflected in milestones and release plans
- Collaborates with Product Lead and Product Manager on trade-offs affecting risk, customer trust, and delivery speed
- Escalates high-risk issues to the Sponsor / Executive Sponsor when policy or business exposure requires approval

---

## Sponsor / Executive Sponsor

### Role Summary
The Sponsor / Executive Sponsor is the senior business owner who approves direction, priorities, and escalations that affect the broader organization. This role provides executive context and helps resolve cross-functional or strategic decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Approve major scope, budget, and business priorities
- Sponsor alignment between project goals and organizational strategy
- Resolve escalations that cross team, product, or business boundaries
- Support stakeholder alignment and executive communication
- Review high-risk or high-impact decisions before launch or major change

### Goals
- Ensure the initiative remains aligned with business value and strategic priorities
- Remove barriers that block successful delivery
- Support a stable, accountable decision-making structure

### Typical Communication
- Executive check-ins and milestone briefings
- Escalation updates for major scope, risk, or business-impact issues
- Leadership alignment on sponsor decisions and strategic trade-offs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Receives updates from the Project Manager and Product Lead on progress, blockers, and major decisions
- Aligns with Product Manager and Product Lead on strategic direction and business trade-offs
- Works with Engineering Manager and Security / Compliance Partner when risk, capacity, or compliance issues require executive action
- Provides final authority for high-impact scope or timeline decisions

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These expanded roles help improve clarity around accountability, responsibilities, and cross-functional collaboration across project work.
- They support better delivery by making ownership, escalation paths, and communication patterns more explicit.
